# Orbit Quiz visual system

## Direction

Orbit Quiz uses a compact mission-console metaphor for astronomy study: a deep ink or pale sky background, matte instrument panels, cyan navigation signals, and yellow solar markers.

## Typography

System sans-serif typography keeps the quiz fast to scan. Large, tightly tracked headings provide a clear moment of orientation; body copy stays between 14 and 16px.

## Composition

The experience is a single narrow column with one question visible at a time. Progress is persistent at the top, answer choices are stacked for thumb and keyboard access, and results use one expressive emoji plus a concise interpretation.

## Motion and states

Entrance motion lifts the console into view. Correct answers use a brief green pop; incorrect answers use a red shake and reveal the correct choice. Reduced-motion preferences disable authored motion.

## Accessibility

Buttons are keyboard-focusable, feedback is announced through a live region, progress uses an accessible progressbar, and color is paired with textual feedback.
