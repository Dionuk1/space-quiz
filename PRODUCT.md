# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students learning astronomy who want a short, approachable way to practice core space-science facts.

## Product Purpose

Space Quiz gives students a focused 10-question practice run with immediate feedback, visible progress, and a clear end-of-quiz result.

## Positioning

A compact, playful “mission” format turns basic astronomy recall into a quick, low-friction study ritual without requiring an account, server, or setup.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One self-contained `index.html` file.
- No server, build step, or external dependencies.
- Ten multiple-choice questions.
- Progress bar, score counter, correct/wrong feedback, and a results screen.
- Must work with keyboard focus and respect `prefers-color-scheme`.

## Product Principles

- Make each question immediately understandable.
- Show progress and consequence without adding pressure.
- Use motion to clarify feedback, not distract from learning.
- Keep the experience lightweight and easy to replay.
